# OzaBay — Premium Motion + Maker Story Upgrade

## Included
- Smooth requestAnimationFrame product-card tilt with lower tilt amplitude and eased return.
- Premium product hover: slower image zoom, moving light/spotlight, restrained lift and deeper shadow.
- New cinematic "Stories that start with the hands" maker-film rail.
- Workshop video playback with muted autoplay/loop, poster fallback and remote-video fallback to existing local OzaBay footage.
- Artisan profile modal upgraded with a workshop-film entry point.
- Maker video modal for viewing a craft film and returning to the maker story.
- Responsive horizontal film rail on mobile and reduced-motion support.
